Meet Joy Rika Miyatake

Joy has been practicing yoga since 2002 and teaching since 2008. A certified Yoga Therapist, her work is rooted in a simple truth: yoga is more than poses—it is lifestyle alignment.

Her studies at Hampshire College and the University of Hawaiʻi explored Eastern spirituality through the lens of Western psychology. Today, she applies that wisdom to modern life, with a special focus on releasing trauma from the body through movement.

Joy has served adaptive sports communities, veterans with PTSD, and Cambodian genocide survivors, helping release the generational effects of war and displacement. Her approach is deeply personal, intuitive, and always centered on what each client truly needs.
